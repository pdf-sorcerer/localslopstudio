The premise

The candidate needs operational leverage, too.

Hiring software gives employers structured data, filters, automation, and analytics. Candidates usually get browser tabs and a spreadsheet. LocalSlop applies product infrastructure to the other side of the funnel: aggregate the market, preserve source truth, reduce repeated work, and make outcomes legible.

Design constraint

Automation can accelerate research and assembly. It cannot invent experience. Every generated claim must remain traceable to a canonical career fact.

System shape

One loop from discovery to outcome.

INGEST→Greenhouse · Lever · Ashby job feeds
NORMALIZE→Canonical roles, companies, locations, dates, descriptions
DECIDE→Search, filters, review state, selected opportunities
TAILOR→Requirement mapping and grounded résumé generation
OBSERVE→Application events, document opens, Gmail outcome signals
